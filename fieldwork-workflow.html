INTERFACE CONCEPT · Planned behavior, not a released feature

Music, with a point of reference

Don’t chase the audio.
Anchor it.

A tempo gives you a starting grid. A sync anchor says exactly where a musical moment belongs in the recording. Add more anchors when a live performance moves around.

01 / IMPORT

Bring in the music.

Import a recording, see the waveform, and define the meter, tempo and marching pulse. A counts-only show works too.

02 / PIN

Find the entrance.

Put the playhead on a downbeat. Choose its measure and beat, or a set arrival. Press Align Here to bind the two moments.

03 / REFINE

Keep the phrase together.

Check a later downbeat and add another anchor. Fit the timing between pins without changing the recording or moving formations.

A concrete example
At quarter = 120 in 4/4, pin measure 1 to 00:12.400. Measure 17 would land at 00:44.400. If the recording arrives at 00:44.600 instead, pin that downbeat and fit the interval. The musical positions stay put; the timing follows the performance.
Guided, not crowded

One useful action.
Then the next.

Choose a field. Add your sections. Bring in music when you’re ready. A short, skippable tour points to the tools and advances as you use them.

Try an editable sample before creating your own show. Reopen the guide from Help whenever you need it.

FIRST-SHOW GUIDE CONCEPT

A file with room to grow

Open doesn’t mean
one-size-fits-all.

The native format keeps the full show together. Exchange formats let it travel. Every conversion should tell you what carries over and what needs a closer look.

FormatPurposePlanned approach
.fieldworkNative, editable showDocumented container with media and show data
OpenMarch .dotsExchange with OpenMarchVersioned adapters with conversion reports
PDF / CSVTeaching and coordinatesReadable exports with field and unit settings
Pyware / UDBPotential future integrationNo compatibility promise at this stage

.dots is OpenMarch’s project format, not a universal drill standard. Import/export must be verified against specific versions. Fieldwork is an independent concept and is not affiliated with OpenMarch, Pyware or Apple.
